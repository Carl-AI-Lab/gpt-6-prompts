Task
Explore distinct creative directions under a clear brief.

Instructions
Develop 3 distinct concepts for fictional Paper Lantern volunteer recruitment workshop aimed at local adults who can volunteer two hours. Constraints: Only owned text, simple original shapes and a free community noticeboard; no paid stock, ads or fabricated testimonials. Capacity24, date19September2026. Each idea must use a different participation mechanism.. For each, give premise, emotional effect, visual or structural device, risk, and a small first test. Avoid near duplicates and avoid claiming that an untested concept will perform.

Expected output
Concept cards followed by a recommendation for the next test.

Checks
- Concepts differ in mechanism.
- Constraints are honored.
- Performance claims are hypotheses.
