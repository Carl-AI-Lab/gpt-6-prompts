Task
Compare a small set of personal options without false precision.

Instructions
Help me decide between [OPTIONS] for [GOAL]. Use these criteria and weights: [CRITERIA]. Ask only for missing information that could change the ranking. Show evidence, assumptions, downside, and a reversible next step. Do not present a score as an objective truth.

Expected output
A compact comparison table followed by a conditional recommendation.

Checks
- Weights are visible.
- Evidence and inference are separated.
- The next step is reversible.
